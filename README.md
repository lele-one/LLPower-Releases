## English

LLPower is a free battery and power monitoring app for Android. It displays power, temperature, voltage, and current in real time, automatically records charging and discharging sessions, and uses machine learning to estimate time to full charge and remaining battery life. All data stays on your device; the app requires no internet permission and contains no ads.

### Key features

- Real-time power, temperature, voltage, and current monitoring
- Automatic charging and discharging history
- Machine-learning estimates for time to full charge and remaining battery life
- Estimated voltage for devices that do not expose voltage readings, plus dual-cell correction
- Home screen widgets and live notification capsules
- Customizable themes and wallpapers
- Local-only data storage with no network permission
